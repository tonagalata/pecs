# Built-in sounds

Short, gentle clips played instead of a word (see `src/lib/sounds.ts`). Chosen and processed to be
child friendly and safe for sound-sensitive users: calm takes (no revving, roaring or alarm bursts),
100 Hz–5 kHz band-pass to remove rumble and harsh highs, 1–1.6 s long, 40 ms fade-in and 250 ms
fade-out, loudness-matched to about -24 dBFS RMS with peaks at or below -10 dBFS (sirens -3 dB lower).
Mono AAC 64 kbps.

All clips are **CC0 / public domain**: free for commercial use, no attribution required.
