# Built-in sounds

Short clips played instead of a word (see `src/lib/sounds.ts`). Each was trimmed to 1–2.5 s,
faded, normalized to -3 dBFS and encoded as mono AAC 64 kbps.

All clips are **CC0 / public domain** — free for commercial use, no attribution required.
